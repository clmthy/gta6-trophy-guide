    Roadmap to the platinum

    Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours (PowerPyx; Push Square says 250+ hours) · every trophy is offline. Play in this order and nothing is wasted.

    1. Phase 1 — Play the story

      Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, and finish the named story missions as they appear (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). That earns the four chapter trophies, the final-mission gold, the opening mission and every named-mission trophy — 12 in all. Story trophies cannot be missed, and missions can be replayed from the mission replay menu after you beat the game.

      Do the Chapter 1 kitchen meditation when you see it — see Missables. It is the one thing that might not come back.

    2. Phase 2 — Strangers, activities and the world

      Work the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret and the rest) — eight full strands earn the Strangers & Freaks silver. Try every activity once, then chase the activity trophies: gold the shooting range, win the drone races and the 20 races, beat Cal at fishing, bowl at Kolt & Associates. Explore Leonida as you go — the map, the vehicles and the WAiNK It locations all count toward later trophies.

    3. Phase 3 — Cleanup to 100%

      Finish what is left: 50 challenges, the card sets, the garage properties, the vehicle registrations, the bank vaults and the marked-bill laundering. Then check the 100% list — 100 objectives in total, including all 16 Strangers & Freaks missions — and close it out for the A Long Road gold. 100% is not missable, so take your time.

    What 100% completion needs

    100 objectives in the game: every story mission, all 16 Strangers & Freaks missions, every side activity and the collectibles that count toward completion. Nothing in 100% is missable. Source: PowerPyx 100% completion checklist.

    Missables

    Good news first: no trophy is confirmed missable. PowerPyx's guide lists no missable trophies, and story missions can be replayed from the mission replay menu after you beat the game.

    ⚠ One possible exception — unconfirmed

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. Push Square flags a Chapter 1 moment: Lucia can meditate in her kitchen in Chapter 1, and Push Square says if you skip it you cannot go back to it later. PowerPyx says meditation is also available in the open world after the story, but does not confirm it is the same meditation. So: do the kitchen meditation the first time you see it in Chapter 1. Do not skip it and hope. Sources: Push Square trophy guide; PowerPyx trophy guide.

    Every other trophy can be earned after the story. If a trophy's guide below says its method is not public yet, that is honest — the game launched 2026-11-19 and some methods are still unconfirmed, not invented here.
